Lisa
Manoban

Lisa, born in Thailand in 1997, is a rapper, singer, and dancer. She joined BLACKPINK in 2016, a highly successful K-pop group. Known for her powerful rap and dance skills, she has also pursued solo projects, including her debut single "LALISA" in 2021. Lisa is a global fashion icon and a beloved member of the group.
