BlackPink

Blackpink, formed in 2016, is a highly successful K-pop group known for their powerful performances and catchy songs. The group consists of four members: Jisoo, Jennie, Rosé, and Lisa. They have a massive global fanbase and have broken numerous records in the music industry.
